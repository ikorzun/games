Art-direction STYLE SHEET for a small video game: one landscape image arranged as a clean presentation board on a plain light warm-grey background, with thin even margins between three zones. Everything on the board is drawn in ONE consistent art style, described at the end.

ZONE A, top-left, the largest panel (about 62% of the width and 56% of the height), in a thin frame: GAME SCREEN KEYFRAME. First-person view from inside a cramped duty-officer booth of a Russian militia (police) station, night, October 1994. We look out through a window protected by vertical steel bars, with a small pass-through slot at the bottom, into a shabby lobby. In the lobby, seen through the bars: a stocky moustached militia sergeant in a grey-blue uniform with red shoulder boards and a peaked cap with a red band holds a detainee by the elbow, a hard-faced man of about 40 in a black leather jacket, with a scar across one eyebrow and a small anchor tattoo on his neck, looking straight at us. Behind them: a wooden bench, a notice board with pinned papers, a round wall clock, a heavy entrance door with dark night outside, one fluorescent tube on the ceiling, walls painted dull green below and whitewashed above. In the near foreground on our side of the bars: a wooden sill with a metal tray holding a dark-red passport, a wallet and a bunch of keys, and the edge of a black rotary telephone.

ZONE B, top-right column: PROPS, six separate objects on the plain board in a tidy grid of two columns and three rows, no frames: a dark-red passport booklet; a worn brown leather wallet with a few banknotes sticking out; a bunch of keys on a ring; a 1990s car cassette radio pulled out of a dashboard, with loose wires; a newspaper cone full of sunflower seeds; a cheap wristwatch.

ZONE C, bottom strip across the full width (about 40% of the height): CHARACTER LINE-UP. Six different full-body characters standing side by side on the plain board, evenly spaced, same scale, feet on one ground line, facing the viewer in relaxed neutral poses, clearly separated from each other:
1. a thin nervous student of 19 with round glasses, a denim jacket and a backpack;
2. a tired factory worker in his 40s with stubble, a flat cap and a grey quilted padded jacket;
3. a small indignant old woman in her 70s in a floral headscarf and an old coat, holding a string bag;
4. a stout moustached cook in a white chef jacket and apron;
5. a slightly drunk middle-aged engineer with glasses, a long unbuttoned overcoat and a crooked tie;
6. a smug early-1990s businessman with a buzz cut, in a crimson blazer with a thick gold chain.
To the right of the line-up: one row of six small square flat colour swatches showing the palette of the sheet.

No text anywhere: no letters, no captions, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature. All characters are original designs; do not depict any existing cartoon, film or game characters.

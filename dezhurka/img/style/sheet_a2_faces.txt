CHARACTER SHEET for a small video game, a continuation of the attached style sheet. Match the art style of the reference exactly (thin, even, slightly wobbly dark-brown contour, flat muted earthy colours with one soft shadow tone, oversized rounded heads with small features in the centre of the face, grounded cinematic lighting, fine film grain) and keep the same character designs and clothes as on the reference.

One landscape image arranged as a clean presentation board on a plain light warm-grey background with thin even margins.

LEFT BLOCK (about 70% of the width): nine framed panels in a grid of three columns and three rows, all exactly the same size, each panel landscape with proportions 3:2 (wider than tall). Each panel is a game frame: one detainee seen from the waist up from the duty officer's window, centred, the head in the upper half of the panel, the same scale in every panel. The same fixed camera and the same background in all nine panels: the shabby lobby of a militia station at night, a wall painted dull green below and whitewashed above, a brown wooden door on the right, a wooden bench with a slatted back against the wall on the left, a cast-iron radiator, a worn linoleum floor, sickly yellow-green light from one fluorescent tube above. No bars, no window frame and no sergeant in these panels.

Top row, the same man in all three panels: the hard-faced man of about 35 with a buzz cut and stubble, in a black leather jacket, with one clearly visible straight scar, a pale-pink line with a thin dark outline that cuts diagonally through his left eyebrow, and a small dark-blue anchor tattoo on the side of his neck. The scar and the tattoo are identical in the three panels. Three states, acted with the whole upper body so that they read at a small size:
1) calm and heavy: square to the camera, chin slightly down, looking straight at us from under his brows;
2) irritated: head turned a little away, eyes looking aside, brows pulled together, jaw pushed forward, one shoulder raised;
3) caught out: head pulled back into the shoulders, brows raised, eyes wide, mouth slightly open, a drop of sweat on the temple.
Middle row, three different people:
4) the small old woman in the floral headscarf and old coat: indignant, chin up, brows lowered, lips pressed, one hand raised with the index finger pointing up;
5) the thin student with round glasses, denim jacket and backpack: frightened, shoulders up, brows raised, mouth open, both hands gripping the backpack straps;
6) the businessman with a buzz cut, in the crimson blazer with a thick gold chain: smug, head tilted back, one eyebrow raised, a small one-sided smile.
Bottom row, three different people:
7) the factory worker with stubble, in a flat cap and a grey quilted padded work jacket: sullen, head lowered, looking at the floor;
8) the stout moustached cook in a white chef jacket: bewildered, brows raised, both palms turned up at chest height;
9) the man in the long unbuttoned overcoat with rectangular glasses and a crooked tie: slightly drunk, flushed cheeks, an ingratiating smile, one hand pressed to his chest.

RIGHT BLOCK (about 30% of the width): the militia sergeant in full body, drawn twice on the plain board at the same scale, feet on one ground line: a front view and a strict side view facing right. A stocky moustached man of about 45, without glasses, in a steel-grey uniform with a bluish tint: a tunic with two chest pockets and one row of five gold buttons, red shoulder boards with three narrow gold stripes across each, a grey shirt and a dark tie, a peaked cap with a red band and a small gold oval badge, a brown leather belt with a holster on his right hip and a black rubber truncheon hanging from the belt on his left hip, trousers with a thin red side stripe, plain high black leather boots without laces. In the side view we see his right side: the holster is visible, the truncheon is hidden behind the body. Bored, patient posture.

DRAWING RULES (strict)
- Eyes: two small round eyes side by side, each a plain white circle with a single outline and a black dot pupil. No line or mark joins the two eyes and no line goes from an eye towards the ear: the eyes must never read as spectacles or a pince-nez. Above each eye there is a short thin eyebrow stroke.
- Glasses are worn only by the student (small round wire glasses, exactly as on the reference) and by the man in the overcoat (small rectangular glasses, as on the reference): two separate lenses with a short bridge line, each eye inside its own lens. Nobody else wears glasses.
- Hands: a thumb and four fingers, each finger a clear separate shape, no extra and no fused fingers.
- The same person looks the same wherever they appear: head shape, hairline, ears, clothes.

No text anywhere: no letters, no captions, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature. All characters are original designs; do not depict any existing cartoon, film or game characters.

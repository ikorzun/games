CHARACTER SHEET for a small video game, a continuation of the attached style sheet. Match the art style of the reference image exactly: the same thin, even, slightly wobbly dark-brown contour, flat muted earthy colours with one soft shadow tone, oversized rounded heads with tiny facial features bunched in the centre of the face, grounded cinematic lighting, fine film grain. Keep the same character designs as on the reference for the scarred man in the black leather jacket, the old woman in the floral headscarf, the student with round glasses, the businessman in the crimson blazer and the moustached sergeant.

One landscape image arranged as a clean presentation board on a plain light warm-grey background with thin even margins.

LEFT BLOCK (about 68% of the width): six framed panels in a grid of three columns and two rows, all the same size, landscape 4:3. Each panel is a game frame: one detainee seen chest-up from the duty officer's window, facing the camera, centred, head in the upper middle of the frame, same scale in every panel, in the same shabby militia station lobby at night (wall painted dull green below and whitewashed above, a brown wooden door on the right, sickly yellow-green fluorescent light from above). No bars, no window frame and no sergeant in these panels.
Top row, the same man in all three panels: a hard-faced man of about 40 in a black leather jacket, with a scar across one eyebrow and a small anchor tattoo on his neck, with three understated expressions: 1) stone-faced and tired, looking straight at us; 2) irritated, jaw set, eyes turned aside; 3) caught out: eyes slightly widened, one drop of sweat on the temple.
Bottom row, three different people: 4) the small old woman in her 70s in a floral headscarf and an old coat, indignant, lips pressed together; 5) the thin student of 19 with round glasses and a denim jacket, frightened, shoulders raised; 6) the smug businessman with a buzz cut, in a crimson blazer with a thick gold chain, with a small confident smile.

RIGHT BLOCK (about 32% of the width): the militia sergeant in full body, drawn twice on the plain board at the same scale, feet on one ground line: a front view and a side view. A stocky moustached man of about 45 in a grey-blue uniform with red shoulder boards, a peaked cap with a red band, a belt with a holster and a rubber truncheon, heavy boots; bored, patient posture.

No text anywhere: no letters, no captions, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature. All characters are original designs; do not depict any existing cartoon, film or game characters.

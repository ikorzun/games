CAST SHEET for a small video game, a continuation of the attached style sheet. Match the art style of the reference exactly: thin, even, slightly wobbly dark-brown contour, flat muted earthy colours with one soft shadow tone, oversized rounded heads with small features in the centre of the face, plain and deadpan.

The setting is the same shabby Russian militia station of 1994, but now it is an INTERGALACTIC duty station: the detainees are aliens and creatures of folklore. They wear ordinary shabby everyday clothes of 1990s townsfolk and stand like tired ordinary people in a queue. Nobody poses; nothing is cute, heroic or scary. The comedy is in how ordinary they look.

One landscape image: a clean presentation board on a plain light warm-grey background. Twelve different full-body characters in two rows of six, evenly spaced, facing the viewer in relaxed neutral poses, feet on one ground line in each row, drawn to one scale (a few are deliberately much smaller or taller than the rest).

Top row, aliens:
1. a small grey-skinned alien with a large bulb-shaped head and big dark almond eyes, in an oversized quilted padded jacket and felt boots, holding a string bag;
2. a tall thin alien with a squid-like face, short tentacles where the mouth would be, in a long grey trench coat and a hat;
3. an insect-like alien with mandibles, antennae and four arms, in a 1990s tracksuit with side stripes;
4. a translucent jelly blob alien with three eyes floating inside it, wearing only a flat cap and a scarf;
5. a stout lizard-skinned alien with a short thick tail, in a crimson blazer with a thick gold chain;
6. a dented boxy robot with one antenna and a single lamp eye, wearing a fur hat with ear flaps and holding a briefcase.

Bottom row, folklore creatures:
7. a forest spirit: an old man covered with moss and bark, with twigs and one small mushroom growing from his head, in a padded work jacket;
8. a water spirit: a fat wet greenish old man with fin-like ears, a long drooping moustache and webbed hands, in a striped sailor vest, standing in a small puddle;
9. a house spirit: a tiny hairy old man half the height of the others, with a huge beard, in a long shirt with a rope belt and felt boots;
10. a small-time devil: short horns, a pig-like snout, a thin tail with a tuft, in a black leather jacket and track pants;
11. a three-headed dragon standing upright like a man: three small heads on long necks coming out of the collar of one overcoat, each head with a different expression;
12. a very thin, very old immortal king: a bald skull-like head with sunken cheeks, a small crown, in a worn grey suit with a row of medal ribbons.

DRAWING RULES (strict)
- Ordinary eyes are small round white circles with a single outline and a black dot pupil, set side by side; no line joins them and no line goes from an eye towards the ear, so they never read as spectacles. Above each eye there is a short thin eyebrow stroke. Nobody wears glasses.
- Hands and paws are drawn clearly: every finger, claw or tentacle is a clear separate shape. Humans and human-like creatures have a thumb and four fingers.
- Each character is clearly separated from its neighbours; no parts merge or overlap between characters.

No text anywhere: no letters, no captions, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature. All characters are original designs; do not depict any existing cartoon, film or game characters.

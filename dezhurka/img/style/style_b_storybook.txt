ART STYLE (apply to every zone): whimsical storybook 2D cartoon. Characters are built from very simple toy-like rounded shapes: a rounded-rectangle or bean-shaped head that merges into a simple tube body, long noodle-thin rubbery arms and legs with no elbows or knees, mitten hands, tiny feet. Faces are reduced to two small black dot eyes set far apart and a small simple line mouth; no nose or just a tiny bump; age is shown only by silhouette, hair and a couple of plain lines. Thin, clean, uniform dark outline. Flat candy-bright pastel colours with no shading on the characters. The background of the keyframe is soft and painterly, with gentle gradients and a light watercolour texture, simplified slightly wonky perspective, softer and more colourful than the characters, with small odd funny details hidden in corners. Even the gloomy police lobby becomes a cosy, colourful fairy-tale version of itself, lit by a warm glow. Props are drawn as simple rounded icons with the same thin outline and flat colour. Mood: naive, sweet, a little weird.

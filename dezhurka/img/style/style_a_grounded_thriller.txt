ART STYLE (apply to every zone): contemporary adult 2D hand-drawn digital animation with a grounded, cinematic, deadpan look, in the European clear-line comics tradition. Character design: oversized, wide, rounded balloon-like heads, each a different simple shape (potato, egg, pear, brick, bean), on compact bodies about four heads tall, with narrow sloping shoulders, soft bellies, stubby limbs, tiny hands and small feet; body types vary from squat to lanky. The facial features are tiny and bunched together in the very centre of the big head: small round eyes with dot pupils set close together, a tiny simple nose, a small thin mouth, leaving large empty areas of forehead, cheek and jaw. Plain, unglamorous, tired, lived-in ordinary people; awkward, understated, naturalistic posture, nobody is posing or mugging. Linework: thin, even, slightly wobbly dark-brown contour of constant width, no thick or tapered ink strokes, no hatching, very few interior lines. Colour: flat fills with one soft shadow tone, no glossy highlights; muted earthy palette of browns, olive, ochre, grey-green and dirty beige with one small saturated accent. The keyframe is lit like a live-action film: motivated light sources, sickly yellow-green fluorescent light inside, cold blue night in the doorway, soft cast shadows, slight haze, fine film grain. The background is carefully observed and realistic in perspective and proportion, full of mundane institutional detail (peeling paint, scuffed linoleum, pipes, wires, a radiator), drawn with the same thin line and flat muted colour. Props are drawn plainly and accurately with the same thin line. Mood: quiet, tense, darkly funny.
